# Assumptions and limitations

## What this project demonstrates

The example models a multi-project research program with quarterly indicators, budgets, commitments, spending, milestones, reporting obligations, research outputs, risks and data-quality issues. The records and metrics are invented to demonstrate analysis and dashboard design.

## Assumptions

- Calendar quarters and fiscal years are used for the demonstration.
- Financial amounts are CAD, fictional and not inflation-adjusted.
- Project budgets are stored by project and fiscal year. Expenditures are transaction-level; commitments remain separate from spending.
- KPI values are comparable only when their definition, unit, direction and period match.
- A reporting obligation is distinct from an observed KPI value. A missing value is not zero.
- The Kaggle country-year data is independent context and is not joined to project facts.
- Example targets and status labels are illustrative, not approved program targets.

## Limitations

- The sample is not a forecast, audited statement, official program record or empirical research result.
- Static PNGs show example chart types; they are not a live Power BI report.
- Country-level energy trends cannot be attributed to this demonstration program.
- Synthetic indicators do not establish causality, additionality, social impact or value for money.
- A live implementation requires source reconciliation, access controls, privacy/security and accessibility review, refresh monitoring and data-owner sign-off.
- The sample quality issue register is illustrative; it does not certify the information.
