## Fictional data and visualizations

All operational rows are generated by `scripts/generate_demo_data.py` with a deterministic seed. Names, organizations, projects, budgets, spending, dates, transactions, indicators, outputs, risks and milestones are invented. No institutional or personal source records were used. PNG charts are generated by `scripts/create_charts.py` from those fictional tables.
