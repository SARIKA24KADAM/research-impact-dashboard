# KPI register

These fictional examples are also written to `data/synthetic/kpi_definitions.csv`.

| Indicator | Unit | Direction | Example use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prototype readiness | Points (0–100) | Higher is better | Compare readiness with a project-specific target |
| Research outputs | Count per quarter | Higher is better | Count recorded outputs; do not treat count alone as impact |
| Partner engagement | Engagements per quarter | Higher is better | Track documented quarterly engagement |
| Energy intensity reduction | Percent | Higher is better | Requires a defined baseline and calculation method |

A real program should define its numerator/denominator where applicable, method, unit, baseline, target, frequency, owner, source, disaggregation, missing-data treatment and revision policy. Demo values are not validated targets or results.
